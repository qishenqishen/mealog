# Mealog Device QA Checklist

## First Launch

- Install a fresh build.
- Confirm onboarding appears once.
- Tap through onboarding and confirm it does not reappear after restart.

## Meal Memory Flow

- Add a Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, and Treats entry.
- Add mood tags, people tags, location, note, and time.
- Reopen Home and confirm the selected date updates immediately.

## Media Persistence

- Add a meal photo from the library.
- Save the meal and fully restart the app.
- Delete the source image from the photo library or computer.
- Reopen Mealog and confirm the image still appears in Detail, Archive, Calendar, and Month Memories.

## People

- Create a person with only a name.
- Add the same person to two meals.
- Confirm Person Detail shows shared history.
- Remove the person from a meal and confirm the profile remains.

## Archive

- Switch Calendar / Memories.
- Change months.
- Tap a day with meals and confirm it opens the right memory.

## Collection

- Confirm keepsake stamps load without broken images.
- Open a locked, in-progress, and unlocked keepsake detail sheet.
- Confirm bottom navigation does not cover the final family section.

## Offline / Restart

- Turn off network.
- Restart the app.
- Confirm existing local meals, people, photos, and keepsakes still load.
